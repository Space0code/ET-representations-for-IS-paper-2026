\section{Conclusion}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Discussion part:
  \item Direct answer to the research question
  \item Frozen learned embeddings: out-of-the-box utility relative to raw and handcrafted alternatives
  \item Representation effect; secondary role of classifier choice
  \item Practical recommendation: accuracy, preprocessing burden, storage, computation, interpretability
  \item Implication of non-improving embeddings: task--pretraining mismatch; need for domain adaptation or fine-tuning
  \item Projection and heatmap: descriptive support only; no separability proof
  \item Persistence result: temporal autocorrelation; distinct from cross-person generalisation
  \item Limitations: single dataset, office-worker sample, self-report noise, binary target construction
  \item Limitations: frozen checkpoints, fixed windowing, quality-control choices, limited subject count
  \item Future work: multi-dataset validation, calibrated personalisation, adaptation/fine-tuning, alternative target formulations

  \item Conclusion part:
  \item Controlled four-representation comparison for unseen-worker engagement inference
  \item Final best-performing representation and headline accuracy
  \item Frozen-encoder conclusion; out-of-the-box deployment recommendation
  \item Concise practitioner guidance: representation choice under limited labelled data
\end{itemize}
